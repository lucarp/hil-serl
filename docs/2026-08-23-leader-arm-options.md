# Leader-arm demos for HIL-SERL — decision brief

## The honest answer

Yes — he can record HIL-SERL demos by moving the leader arm, and there are two working routes to it, but neither is free and neither retires the gamepad. Everything upstream of the dataset is solvable: the leader's joint positions FK cleanly into the EE-delta action space (`model/kinematics.py:74-96`, verified against his own `assets/SO-ARM100/Simulation/SO101/so101_new_calib.urdf` and his own 30-episode dataset — 10 Hz per-step EE displacement mean 9.1 mm / p95 25 mm, so only 2.5% of deltas clip at `end_effector_step_sizes=0.02`; his demos genuinely fit the action space). What he cannot escape is the second half of HIL-SERL: `make_robot_env` asserts a teleop exists and `make_processors` builds `AddTeleopEventsAsInfoStep(teleop_device=...)` unconditionally (`rl/gym_manipulator.py:335, 488`), whose `__post_init__` raises `TypeError` for any device lacking `get_teleop_events` (`processor/hil_processor.py:147-149`) — and without a trained reward classifier the *online reward signal is literally the gamepad success button* (`hil_processor.py:489, 517`). So: leader-arm **demo recording** is 5-16 hours of work depending on route; leader-arm **interventions and online reward during training** are solved by exactly one of those routes, the expensive one. Condition: he must supply a success/terminate event source with his hands on the leader (keyboard or footswitch), because `next.reward` has no fallback in the offline converter (`rl/buffer.py:671` — unguarded `float(current_sample["next.reward"].item())`), and he must assert intervention on every frame or `gym_manipulator.py:699` records the neutral `[0,0,0,1]` action and the dataset is structurally valid and worthless.

## Recommended path

**This week: record run 1's demos with the gamepad. Start training. Do not build tooling first.**

That is not defeat, and here is exactly why:

1. **No leader route removes the gamepad from training.** Options 2, 4 and 5 solve demo recording only. Interventions during an online HIL-SERL run are *hours* of gamepad flying spread across the whole session; demo recording is minutes. Spending 5-8 h to eliminate the small half while leaving the large half untouched is the worst trade on the table.
2. **The demo set is tiny.** The reference dataset `lilkm/pick_cube_franka_panda_30` is 458 frames across 30 episodes at 10 Hz — about 15 frames, 1.5 s, per episode. Even at ten times that length, 20-30 demos is well under an hour on the pad, and he already has gamepad interventions working from MuJoCo.
3. **Run 1's gamepad dataset is the reference schema he validates everything else against.** Without one, he has nothing to diff a converted or leader-recorded dataset against, and every schema bug (image geometry, `observation.state` width, gripper class encoding) becomes an ambiguous training failure instead of a `assert features == features` mismatch.
4. **Tooling-first risks poisoning the first run.** If demos come from a converter he wrote yesterday and training stalls, he cannot tell whether HIL-SERL is failing on hyperparameters or on his own silently-wrong data. Get one known-good baseline first.

**Then, while the learner runs: build option 3 as his second upstream PR.** It is the only option that is both a real ergonomic win (demos *and* interventions on the leader) and a real CV item. Do the 1-2 h IK fix first (below) — it changes how the finished thing feels, and it improves the gamepad path too, so it is worth doing even if the teleop class slips.

**Read PR #3878 before building option 3, but do not adopt it.** It is the best available reference for the FK→delta math and it has already been sanity-checked against his hardware config. Post the two API-drift fixes as a comment on that PR — 10 minutes, keeps his name on a thread maintainers will eventually read.

## If he builds it — `SOLeaderEndEffector`

**Files.** Create `vendor/lerobot/src/lerobot/teleoperators/so_leader/so_leader_end_effector.py` (~230 lines) and `.../config_so_leader_end_effector.py`. Edit `.../so_leader/__init__.py` (export both — this alone is sufficient for draccus *and* the factory, which falls through to `make_device_from_device_class` at `teleoperators/utils.py:114-118` → `utils/import_utils.py:149-189`; both paths verified). Add an explicit `elif config.type == "so_leader_ee"` branch in `teleoperators/utils.py` anyway — not needed to work, but it is what maintainers will want. Add a 3-line `reset()` to `AddTeleopActionAsComplimentaryDataStep` in `processor/hil_processor.py` forwarding to the device (the pipeline already calls `step.reset()` at every episode boundary via `pipeline.py:1604-1608` ← `gym_manipulator.py:603`). Update the error message at `hil_processor.py:88-90` to list the new class. Add `tests/teleoperators/test_so_leader_end_effector.py` using the existing `tests/mocks/mock_feetech.py`.

**Interface (exact).** `class SOLeaderEndEffector(SOLeader)` — subclassing inherits the bus, `connect(calibrate=True)`, `calibrate`, `configure`, `disconnect`, `is_connected`, `is_calibrated`, and critically `name = "so_leader"` (`so_leader.py:37`), which keeps calibration resolving to the existing `~/.cache/huggingface/lerobot/calibration/teleoperators/so_leader/leader_arm.json`. Setting `name` yourself triggers a full interactive re-calibration on first connect. Provide:

- `action_features` → `{"dtype":"float32","shape":(4,),"names":{"delta_x":0,"delta_y":1,"delta_z":2,"gripper":3}}` — the **dataset-spec** form (mirror `teleop_gamepad.py:70-82`), never `SOLeader`'s `{f"{motor}.pos": float}` (`so_leader.py:56-58`), which `KeyError`s on `ft["dtype"]` inside `LeRobotDatasetMetadata.create` (`dataset_metadata.py:391-393`).
- `feedback_features` → `{}`; override `send_feedback` to a no-op (the inherited one writes `Goal_Position` and would fight the operator's hand).
- `get_action()` → flat dict of those 4 keys. Deltas **dimensionless in [-1,1]**, not metres — `EEReferenceAndDelta` multiplies by `end_effector_step_sizes` downstream (`robot_kinematic_processor.py:126-134`) and SAC learns that same normalized space. Gripper is the class index `{0.0, 1.0, 2.0}`, hysteretic; feeding the raw 0-100 leader gripper makes `GripperVelocityToJoint` compute `-(50-1)*clip_max` and slam the jaw every frame (`:418`).
- `get_teleop_events()` → dict keyed by the `TeleopEvents` **enum members**. It runs immediately after `get_action` each step (`gym_manipulator.py:487-488`), so serve a cached bus read — do not hit the CH343 twice.
- `reset()` (non-ABC) clearing `_prev_ee` and `_residual`.

**The delta math — settled.** Emit **per-step FK differences of the leader's own tip**, with a residual accumulator for clipped motion, a ~1 mm deadband (one encoder count is 0.45-0.48 mm at the tip, i.e. 24× `MapDeltaActionToRobotActionStep`'s 1e-3 threshold), and a >5 cm teleport guard for repositioning. Do *not* use leader-minus-follower here: I read the code to settle a contradiction between two of the research passes. `EEReferenceAndDelta` is built with `use_latched_reference=False, use_ik_solution=True` (`gym_manipulator.py:503-508`), and with `use_ik_solution` the reference joints come from `complementary_data["IK_solution"]` (`robot_kinematic_processor.py:87-88`), written at `:629` and carried forward across steps by `gym_manipulator.py:566, 581-588`. **The reference is FK of the previous IK solution — a pure command integrator, re-seeded from the measured robot only at episode reset (`gym_manipulator.py:603-609`).** The follower's measured pose never enters the loop, so per-step leader differences are the faithful inversion, and a teleop class (which has no handle on the follower anyway) is structurally correct here.

**Events with both hands on the leader.** One device serves both motion and events — `make_robot_env` builds exactly one teleop and hands the same object to both steps (`gym_manipulator.py:335-339, 486-491`), and there is no composite teleop anywhere in the tree (`teleoperators/utils.py:36-118`, `envs/configs.py:310`, and `lerobot_record.py:272-277` gates its `list[Teleoperator]` branch to `lekiwi_client`). So the class owns its own `pynput` listener, reusing `KeyboardTeleop`'s machinery (`teleop_keyboard.py:56-135`); pynput 1.8.2 is installed and `pynput_can_capture()` returns True on his X11 session. Success/failure/rerecord are once-per-episode so a hand can leave the leader, but a ~$20 USB footswitch that emits keystrokes drops into the same code with zero changes and is what makes this pleasant — budget for one. For record mode set `always_intervene=True` so `IS_INTERVENTION` is true every frame; for training interventions use a space-bar toggle.

**Do the IK fix first (1-2 h, separate PR).** `RobotKinematics.inverse_kinematics` calls `self.solver.solve(True)` exactly once (`model/kinematics.py:132`). Measured consequence: roughly 5-10% of every commanded step is lost per step and, because the reference integrates the *achieved* commanded pose, never recovered — actual/commanded ≈ 0.91-0.94 early, collapsing toward 0.4-0.8 near the workspace edge. Without this the leader is a comfortable 3-DOF rate handle that steadily drifts behind his hand, not a mirror. A `num_iters` loop fixes it (his own notes record convergence 0.042 → 0.0072 → 0.0002) and improves the gamepad path identically.

**Effort: 12-16 h.** 10-14 h for the class, tests and bench-check; 1-2 h for the IK PR; 1 h config plumbing.

**PR story — unusually strong.** `docs/source/hilserl.mdx:474-500` already documents this exact workflow (`--teleop.type=so101_leader`, `control_mode: "leader"`, "use a keyboard to press s for success"), and the code no longer delivers it: `control_mode` is declared at `envs/configs.py:294` and read *nowhere*, and `SOLeader` fails the events protocol. Likely regressed by the RL-stack refactor (#3075). So the PR is "restore a documented, regressed workflow", not "add my pet feature" — the strongest framing available. Bundle: the class + config + tests + the `hil_processor.py` error-message and `reset()` changes + a docs fix that either wires up or deletes the dead `control_mode` field. Branch off upstream, **not** off `local/hilserl` (which must never be pushed), same pattern as `fix/reset-config-joint-positions-type`. Note that issue #2952 is still open with his own 2026-08-02 analysis as its most complete comment and no maintainer has ever replied — expect to own this locally for a while regardless of merge.

## Traps

- **`next.reward` is mandatory and unguarded.** `buffer.py:671` does `float(current_sample["next.reward"].item())` with no existence check. A demo dataset without it dies with `KeyError` before a single gradient step. `lerobot-record` never writes it (`lerobot_record.py:394-407`), and `utils/keyboard_input.py` has no success key — only end/re-record/stop.
- **A dataset of neutral actions looks perfect and is worthless.** If `IS_INTERVENTION` is not true on every frame, `gym_manipulator.py:699` writes `[0,0,0,1]` and the recorded `complementary_data["teleop_action"]` (`:720`) carries no signal. Nothing warns.
- **fps is never validated.** Nothing in `make_dataset`, the buffer, or the SAC config compares the offline dataset's fps to `env.fps`, and the SAC actor declares no `delta_timestamps` (`configuration_gaussian_actor.py:216-226`). A 30 Hz demo set trains silently with dt 3× too short and actions 3× too small. Record at 10 Hz.
- **`use_gripper=False` is broken pipeline-wide.** `MapTensorToDeltaActionDictStep` only adds the `"gripper"` key when True (`delta_action_processor.py:55-56`) but `MapDeltaActionToRobotActionStep` does `action.pop("gripper")` with no default (`:100`) → `KeyError` on step 1. Keep it True in both the teleop and `env.processor.gripper`.
- **`use_degrees` must stay True.** FK does `np.deg2rad` (`kinematics.py:74-96`), but `use_degrees=False` switches the bus to `RANGE_M100_100` (`so_leader.py:41`) and FK reads those as degrees. Silent.
- **His gripper sign is probably inverted relative to lerobot's assumption.** In his own data, 86% of large tracking-error frames show the follower stalled *above* the command (commanded median 0.3, follower median 21.4) — jaws only stall while closing, so `gripper.pos → 0` is CLOSED on his arm, while `GripperVelocityToJoint` maps class 0 (close) to `+clip_max` (`:418`). Bench-check this in 2 minutes before writing any gripper code; getting it backwards commands a full-travel close onto whatever is between the jaws. His leader's gripper spans 0..60.5, so `max_gripper_pos` should be ~60, not the 100.0 default (`envs/configs.py:301`) or the 30.0 buried in `GripperPenaltyProcessorStep` (`hil_processor.py:369`).
- **The gripper penalty is effectively dead as shipped** — `hil_processor.py:402-403` divides the *discrete class* (0/1/2) by `max_gripper_pos`, so `cmd_close (>0.9)` can essentially never fire. Emitting zeros for `complementary_info.discrete_penalty` is fine; just do not spend a day thinking you mis-converted it.
- **But do emit that column.** `concatenate_batch_transitions` (`buffer.py:813-829`) merges only over keys present in the offline batch; omitting it once online intervention transitions land in the same buffer gives a `discrete_penalty` of `n_online` rows against `batch_size` rewards, which broadcasts to `(batch, batch)` inside the discrete-critic loss (`rl/algorithms/sac/sac_algorithm.py:375-377`). Silent corruption.
- **Local datasets may never be finalized.** `gym_manipulator.py:770-774` calls `dataset.finalize()` only inside the `if cfg.dataset.push_to_hub:` branch; otherwise it relies on `DatasetWriter.__del__`. Unfinalized parquet has no footer and `__getitem__` raises (`lerobot_dataset.py:474-482, 513-514`). Verify or set `push_to_hub=true`.
- **Image geometry is baked in at record time and irreversible.** The learner's offline path applies no crop/resize; a mismatch surfaces as a matmul error deep in `SpatialLearnedEmbeddings` (`modeling_gaussian_actor.py:172-186`). PR #3878's crop is plain numpy slicing which *silently clamps* out-of-range indices — its shipped 1280×720 crop `[0,70,720,720]` applied to 640×480 frames yields a 480×570 crop with no error. Record uncropped first, then use `rl/crop_dataset_roi.py` (but do not copy its episode loop: `:231-236` adds the frame before the boundary check, appending each episode's first frame to the previous episode).
- **The leader will snap the follower on the first `send_action`.** PR #3878's `create_robot()` sets no `max_relative_target`, and its startup gripper-confirm loop is deceptively safe (it sends the follower's own arm joints, `:510-511`) — the instant Enter is pressed, `:563` sends the full leader pose uncapped. Align first, or set `max_relative_target`.
- **`EEBoundsAndSafety(raise_on_jump=True)` is the default** and `gym_manipulator.py:510` does not override it — a `ValueError: EE jump ... > 0.05m` kills a live episode mid-recording. Reproduced even with correctly clipped deltas near joint limits, and his existing data has 10 Hz steps up to 50.2 mm, exactly on the 0.05 limit.
- **`pynput` listens globally.** Whatever window has focus also receives `q`/`r`/`s`. Record with one bare terminal focused.
- **Orientation is structurally unavailable.** `MapDeltaActionToRobotActionStep` hardcodes `target_wx/wy/wz = 0.0` (`delta_action_processor.py:114-116`). Rotating the leader wrist produces a *translation* of the tip, not a rotation of the follower's. Demonstrate with wrist orientation roughly fixed. Measured on his data: the same tip path is reached through joints diverging 2-3° by step 3 and 12-30° by end of episode.
